iMiqaat

Salah times on your Mac's Lock Screen.

Download for Mac

Free and open source. macOS 13 or later. Pre-release.

Sample times for Makkah. The wallpaper is a stand-in.

What it does

  • The next prayer, with a live countdown, right on the Lock Screen.
  • Your Mac's location, or a city of your choice in its own time zone.
  • Today's times: Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha, with the Hijri date, your mosque's iqamah times and Jumu'ah on Fridays.
  • Your choice of calculation method, including Umm al-Qura, ISNA and JAKIM, plus the Asr madhab and high-latitude rule.
  • Lives in the menu bar, with no Dock icon, and can start at login.

Privacy

Prayer times are calculated on your Mac. iMiqaat uses your location only for that calculation and never sends it anywhere. It works offline.

If you choose a city instead, the search sends what you type to Apple Maps.

Installing

With Homebrew: brew install --cask u-sm/tap/imiqaat. Or download the DMG.

Pre-releases aren't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS stops the app the first time you open it:

  1. Open the DMG and drag iMiqaat to Applications (Homebrew does this for you).
  2. Open iMiqaat. When macOS says it can't verify the developer, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to iMiqaat.
